Idle Crypto Tycoon

Start with an old PC, automate mining, and build your idle crypto empire.

Turn basic hardware into an advanced virtual mining operation. Upgrade miners, automate production, progress while offline, and prestige for permanent bonuses. Idle Crypto Tycoon is a virtual simulation game and does not mine or reward real cryptocurrency.

✨ Key Features

⚡ Offline Progress

Your operation keeps producing while you're away. Return to collect offline earnings and reinvest them in stronger miners and upgrades.

🔄 Progression & Prestige

Reset your progress when growth slows, earn Prestige Tokens, and unlock permanent bonuses that make every new run faster.

🤖 Strategic Automation

Unlock scripts to automate production. Progress from basic computers and smartphones to mining rigs, ASICs, data centers, and the Quantum Miner.

🎮 Missions & Random Events

Complete quick missions, collect delivery bonuses, face hacker attacks, and discover random events that add variety to every session.

💎 Play Your Way

Progress through regular play, use rewarded ads to improve selected rewards, or choose optional in-app purchases to accelerate parts of your journey.

☁️ Cloud Save & Updates

Sync your progress across supported devices with Google Play Games cloud save and enjoy regular updates, improvements, and balance changes.

⛏️ Why Play

  • Visible Progression: Start with an old PC and unlock increasingly powerful miners
  • Idle Automation: Use scripts and upgrades to keep your operation producing
  • Permanent Growth: Prestige for bonuses that make every new run stronger
  • More Ways to Progress: Complete missions, collect deliveries, face events, and unlock skins
